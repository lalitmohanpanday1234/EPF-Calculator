📌 Quick Rules (India)

  • Employee contribution: 12% of Basic + DA
  • Employer contribution: 12% of Basic + DA — split into EPS (8.33%, capped at ₹1,250/month) and EPF (remaining)
  • EDLI: 0.5% of basic (max ₹75/month) — paid by employer
  • Admin charges: 0.5% of basic (max ₹75/month) — paid by employer
  • Statutory wage ceiling: ₹15,000/month for EPS & EDLI
  • Current EPF interest rate: 8.25% p.a. (FY 2024-25)
